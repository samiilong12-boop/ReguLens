2. **Regulatory Tracks for Fresh Egyptian Strawberries (`CN 0810 10 00`):**

They require pre-notification via **CHED-D**, **100% documentary checks** (Art 54(1) of Reg 2017/625 [Not verified from a saved primary text]), and a single combined frequency of **20% for identity and physical checks** (Art 6 of Reg 2019/1793).

3. **Regulatory Tracks for Frozen Egyptian Strawberries (`CN 0811 10`):**
   - *Statutory Examination of Annex XI & Critical Version Caution:* CN 0811 10 is not identified in the examined Annex XI entries. Conclusions depending on the current Annex XI require verification against the applicable consolidated version (`REQUIRES HUMAN VERIFICATION`). The absence of CN 0811 10 from the original 2019 Annex XI cannot be presented as proof of its absence from the current Annex XI. ## 5. Claims Corrected or Contradicted
